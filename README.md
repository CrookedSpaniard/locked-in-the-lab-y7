# Locked in the Lab · Year 7 escape room

A 30-minute escape room for Year 7 (teams of 2 or 3, one device per team) for the British School Alzira · Xàtiva · Gandia Group.

The computing lab door is locked. Seven locks, one per idea from the unit, each give a letter; the letters spell **RESPECT** and open the door.

1. **R · Password Lock**: the strongest password, good and bad password advice, build a password from three random words.
2. **E · Lab Safety Lock**: find 7 hazards in the lab, choose the lab rules.
3. **S · Email Lock**: respectful or not, fix Mia’s email, who can read school emails.
4. **P · Comment Lock**: kind and helpful comments, the feedback sandwich, sarcasm online.
5. **E · Feedback Lock**: Read, Stop, Appreciate, Decide, Remember.
6. **C · Cyberbullying Lock**: what cyberbullying is, stop/speak/support.
7. **T · Slide Lock**: true or false about slides, the better slide, images labelled for reuse.

Points: 100 per task with no hints, 70 with hint 1, 40 with hint 2, 10 with “Help us through” (shows the answers); each mistake takes away 10 points down to a floor for that level. Every task ends with a “Remember” explanation. A certificate with points and stars, a short team promise, and a results report that pupils copy into a private comment in Google Classroom. Progress is saved on the device and can be moved to another day or device with a progress code (💾 Save / 🔑 Continue with a code).

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Content lives in `js/data.js` (tasks) and `js/extra.js` (intros, second hints and explanations).

Based on the Teach Computing Curriculum (National Centre for Computing Education · Raspberry Pi Foundation), Year 7 “Impact of technology – Collaborating online respectfully”, lessons 1–5, Open Government Licence v3.0.
